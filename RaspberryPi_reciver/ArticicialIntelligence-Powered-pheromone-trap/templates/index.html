Project Overview

This research project focuses on monitoring insect populations using IoT-enabled pheromone traps deployed in agricultural fields. Images are captured using ESP32-CAM devices and processed locally on a Raspberry Pi for long-term storage, analysis, and decision support.

Why This Matters

  • Early detection of pest population growth
  • Reduced dependence on excessive pesticide spraying
  • Support for integrated pest management strategies
  • Reliable datasets for agricultural and entomological research
